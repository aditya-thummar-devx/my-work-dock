Help and FAQ

Find your problem below. If it is not here, open an issue.

Opening the app

macOS says the app can’t be opened or can’t be verified

Expected on first launch. Follow Get past the privacy blocker.

There is no “Open Anyway” button in Privacy & Security

It only shows for a while after a blocked launch. Double-click the app again, cancel the warning, and go straight back to System Settings › Privacy & Security. Or use the one-line Terminal fix on the privacy blocker page.

I opened it but I do not see anything

There is no Dock icon. Look at the top of the screen for the dark bar around the notch and at the menu-bar icon. If you chose Hide MyWorkDock from the menu-bar icon, choose Show. You can also press ⌥ Space.

It will not run on my Mac

MyWorkDock needs macOS 14 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac. Intel Macs are not supported.

Shortcuts and display

A hotkey does nothing
  • Another app may already use that key combination. MyWorkDock skips the combination it cannot claim; the others still work. Change the other app’s shortcut or use the notch instead.
  • The hotkey’s menu may be switched off in Settings › Menus.
  • Confirm the app is running (menu-bar icon visible).
The bar does not show on my external display

Turn on Settings › Menus › Show on all displays. Then hover or click the bar on the display you want to use.

Tab or Shift+Tab does not switch tabs

They are ignored while the cursor is in a text field. Press Esc to leave the field first.

Clipboard

Clipboard history stays empty
  • If macOS asked about Paste from Other Apps and you chose Don’t Allow, allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Paste from Other Apps. The Clipboard footer also tells you when this is the cause.
  • Text copied while a password manager is the front app is never recorded, by design.
  • Only text is stored. Images and files are not.
  • Check that Clipboard is switched on in Settings › Menus.
An old clip disappeared

Only the last 200 untagged, unpinned clips are kept by default. Tag or pin a clip to keep it forever, or raise the limit in Settings › Clipboard.

My Calendar

Google says “Access blocked” when I sign in
  • My Calendar only accepts devxlabs.ai accounts. Pick that account in Google’s account chooser; a personal Gmail account is turned away.
  • If your Workspace admin restricts third-party apps, Google blocks the sign-in until they trust MyWorkDock (Admin console › Security › Access and data control › API controls). Send them the error text.
The tab says “Session expired”

Google no longer accepts the saved sign-in. That happens after a password reset, when access was revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or when your admin changes a policy. Press Sign in with Google again.

“Google sent no refresh token”

Google only hands out a lasting sign-in on a fresh approval. Remove MyWorkDock at myaccount.google.com/permissions, then sign in again.

“Could not reach Google”, or the footer shows an error

Check your connection. The tab keeps showing the events it already has and tries again when you press the refresh button, change the range or reopen the panel. Events are only cached for a minute.

An event is missing
  • Open the calendar button in the header and make sure that event’s calendar is ticked.
  • Declined events are shown struck through, not hidden. Workspace “working location” entries are not shown.
  • Press the refresh button: the panel keeps what it fetched for a minute.
“Sign-in is not set up” or “this build’s OAuth client” errors

You are running a build that was made without Google’s sign-in details, or with wrong ones. Official releases include them. If you build from source, follow the “Build from source” note in the README, and make sure the Google Calendar API is switched on for your Google Cloud project.

claudex and Emulator

The claudex tab says “claudex is not available” or shows a ⚠

The tab drives the claudex command-line tool, so it must be installed and on your PATH. Install it, then press Retry on that screen. A ⚠ with raw text means claudex printed something this version does not recognise; update claudex or MyWorkDock.

No iOS simulators, or no Android emulators, are listed

iOS needs Xcode so that xcrun simctl works. Android needs the Android SDK with adb and emulator, usually at ~/Library/Android/sdk. Press the refresh button after installing.

Wireless pairing says “No route to host”

MyWorkDock needs the Local Network permission to reach your phone. Grant it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network, then press Restart adb server in Emulator › Wireless and try again.

Where your data lives

Everything is stored on your Mac and nowhere else:

File (in ~/Library/Application Support/MyWorkDock/)Holds
clipboard.jsonClipboard history. Plain text, so treat it like any file on your disk.
notes.jsonTODOs and tag colours
commands.json, prompts.json, links.jsonYour saved snippets
wireless-android.jsonKnown wireless Android devices
google-calendar.jsonYour Google sign-in for My Calendar, in a file only you can read. Delete it, or use Sign out, to forget the sign-in. Calendar events themselves are never saved.

Settings (theme, tint, panel size, switched-off menus) are in the app’s macOS preferences, com.adityabuilds.myworkdock. To back up your notes, copy the folder above.

Uninstall

  1. Quit MyWorkDock from the menu-bar icon.
  2. If you turned on Open at login, turn it off first (right-click the notch bar › Open at login) or remove it in System Settings › General › Login Items.
  3. Delete MyWorkDock from Applications.
  4. Optional, to remove your data too: delete ~/Library/Application Support/MyWorkDock, and run defaults delete com.adityabuilds.myworkdock to clear settings.
