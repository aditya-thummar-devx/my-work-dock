Usage

MyWorkDock lives at the top of your screen, around the notch. It has no Dock icon and stays out of the way until you want it.

The four states

StateWhat you seeHow to get there
IdleA black bar the exact size of the notch. On Macs without a notch (and on external displays) a small pill at top centre.Default.
PeekThe bar widens to show your TODO and clipboard counts on the left and your local IP on the right. Switch claudex on in Settings › Peek to show your Claude usage rings instead. With nothing to show it says “Tap to see magic”.Move the pointer onto it.
LiveA bar drops down with a progress line while an iOS simulator or Android emulator boots.Starts by itself when a boot begins.
OpenThe full panel with the four menus.Click the bar, or use a hotkey.

The panel closes by itself about a third of a second after the pointer leaves it. A panel you opened with a hotkey stays until the pointer has entered it at least once. Clicks outside the drawn shape pass straight through to the apps underneath.

Closing, pinning and moving around

  • Pin (the pin icon in the header) keeps the panel open when the pointer leaves.
  • Collapse (bottom right) closes the panel and unpins it.
  • Esc closes the panel. If you are editing, searching or looking at a confirmation, Esc cancels that first.
  • The sliders icon opens Settings. The header also shows your local IP; click it to copy.

Global hotkeys

These work from any app. Each opens the panel straight on that tab.

KeysAction
⌥ SpaceOpen or close the panel
⌘ ⇧ VClipboard
⌘ ⇧ TNotes › TODO
⌘ ⇧ PNotes › Prompts
⌘ ⇧ LNotes › Links
⌘ ⇧ EEmulator
⌘ ⇧ Xclaudex

A hotkey does nothing if its menu is switched off in Settings. If another app already uses the same combination, that one hotkey is skipped and the rest still work.

Inside the panel

KeysAction
TabNext menu (Clipboard, Notes, claudex, Emulator)
⇧ TabNext sub-tab of the current menu (for example TODO → Commands → Prompts → Links)
⌘ FSearch in Clipboard, TODO, Prompts and Links
EnterAdd an item, or save an inline edit
⇧ EnterNew line while writing a prompt
EscCancel an edit or search, then close the panel

Tab and ⇧ Tab do not switch tabs while you are typing in a text field.

Copy with a double-click.

In Clipboard, Commands, Prompts and Links, a single click only shows a hint. Double-click an item to copy it, or use its Copy button.

Menu-bar icon and right-click menu

  • Menu-bar icon: Show or Hide MyWorkDock, Check for Updates…, Quit.
  • Right-click the notch bar: Open at login, Check for Updates…, Quit.

Several displays

By default one bar sits on your notch display (or the display under the pointer). Turn on Settings › Menus › Show on all displays to draw it on every display. Hover or click the one you want to use.
